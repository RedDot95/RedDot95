## Finding format

- **ID / title:**
- **Reviewed commit and affected code:** path, function, exact line range/link.
- **Status:** confirmed, unconfirmed hypothesis or informational.
- **Severity and rationale:** impact, prerequisites, attacker capability and practical feasibility.
- **Root cause:** code-level explanation.
- **Reproduction:** minimal steps/test, environment and observed vs expected behavior; explicitly state if not run.
- **Impact:** affected assets/users/privileges and realistic boundaries.
- **Recommendation:** concrete mitigation and tradeoffs.
- **Project response:** quoted/attributed only with permission.
- **Retest:** exact new commit, commands, result and residual issues; otherwise not retested.

## Open questions and coverage

List unresolved design questions, reviewed surfaces and omitted checks. A report describes the work actually performed; absence of findings is not proof of safety.
